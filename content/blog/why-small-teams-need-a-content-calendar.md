# Why small studios need a content calendar

Most studio owners post when they remember to. Then a busy week comes, nothing goes out for a
fortnight, and the next post is a rushed "we're still here!".

A content calendar fixes that. It's a simple plan of what you'll post, where and when.

## Start with one month

- Pick two channels you already use. For most studios that's Instagram and email.
- Choose one post a week per channel. Consistency beats volume.
- Write the topics down before the month starts.

## Use what's already happening

Your timetable is full of ideas: a new teacher, a workshop, a class that's always full.
Put those dates in the calendar first, then fill the gaps.

## Review it every Friday

Ten minutes: what went out, what didn't, what's next week. That's all it takes to stay on track.
