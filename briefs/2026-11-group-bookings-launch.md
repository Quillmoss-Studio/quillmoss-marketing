# Brief: Group bookings launch (November)

- **What:** Quillmoss's new group bookings feature. Studios can let one client book a class for
  up to 6 friends in a single checkout.
- **Launch date:** 12 November
- **Audience:** owners and managers of small yoga and fitness studios already using Quillmoss
- **Goal:** 300 studios switch the feature on in the first 30 days
- **Channels:** blog post, 3 LinkedIn posts, in-app message, email to customers
- **Key message:** Fill more spots in every class without more admin.
