# Quillmoss Studio: marketing

This is where the Quillmoss marketing team keeps its work: briefs, copy, campaign plans and brand
rules. Every change is saved, and nothing becomes final until it's approved.

> Quillmoss Studio is a fictional company used for training videos.

## How to ask for work

Open an **issue** (Issues tab, New issue). Say what you need, for which channel, the goal and
the deadline. One request per issue.

## How work gets approved

1. Make a **branch** for your draft.
2. Save your changes as **commits**, with a short note each time.
3. Open a **pull request** and link the issue.
4. The owner of that area reviews it, comments, and approves.
5. The approved pull request is **merged** into `main`, the official version.

## Who owns what

| Folder | What's in it | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `brand/` | Voice, messaging, visual rules | Brand lead |
| `briefs/` | Approved briefs | Marketing manager |
| `campaigns/` | One folder per campaign: plan, dates, results | Campaign owner |
| `content/blog/` | Blog posts | Content lead |
| `content/social/` | LinkedIn, Instagram and other social copy | Social lead |

## What doesn't go here

Videos, photos and design files stay in Google Drive, Canva or Figma. Put the link in the
issue or the file instead.
